# CBL VIctory results

## Gameplay · 26 November 2026

Tested on Windows with a Ryzen 9 9950X3D and GeForce RTX 5080, using the Series X profile. Build `cbaef90` presented at 3840×2160. The output size does not establish the internal resolution of individual passes.

| Area | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| Progress | The repeat passed the cashier, reached the exterior under player control and continued into Chapter 1. The boat engine responded to input but the boat did not move far enough to complete the objective. |
| Picture | Walls, characters and ceiling fixtures render. The cashier can become severely overexposed. Outdoors, shadows are very dark and some surfaces appear distorted or low-detail. |
| Speed | The first run averaged 5.58 FPS over 326 measured seconds on the moving route and 5.95 FPS over 36 seconds at the cashier. The repeat averaged 4.88 FPS over 428 seconds approaching the cashier, 3.63 FPS over 135 seconds after it, and 6.39 FPS over a 31-second boat segment. |
| Stability | The first run crashed at 20 min 11 s uptime. The repeat passed that point in the sequence and ran for 27 min 38 s before manual close, about 25 minutes after first gaining control, including cutscenes and retries. |

FPS is the number of successful host-present intervals divided by their total elapsed time. For exact run commands, screenshot timings, binary identities and CPU/GPU benchmarks, see the [run notes](docs/cbl/tracker-run.md).
