## Robbery/cashier scene and current RT decision, November 25

`threaten-cashier.png` (15:04:44 Sydney time) shows the objective “Grab the cash” and subtitle “Take it, that's all there is.” The window title reports 8 fps, 127.2 ms/frame and 4,133 draws. The cashier counter and at least one character have visible color/texture; the exterior sky and broad opening are heavily overexposed, a large overhead surface is nearly solid black, the counter is strongly blown out, and the image has pronounced blur and red/cyan fringes. The image establishes story progression and substantial scene rendering; it does not establish that the scene's geometry, exposure or lighting is correct.

The log around this capture is highly variable with scene load. At 15:04:02–15:04:24 it reports about 4.8–5.0 fps, 200–210 ms/frame, roughly 8,300–8,500 draws, and 87–97 ms on the GPU. At 15:04:35–15:05:02 it ranges from 5.6 to 8.3 fps, 120–179 ms/frame and 4,300–4,500 draws, with 26–48 ms on the GPU in most samples. Preparation and copy-back remain substantial. These samples are not a controlled performance comparison; they suggest workload and copy/prepare cost change as the scene transitions. The screenshot and latest log continue to show no device-removal entry in the inspected tail.

### Updated hypotheses

- **The cashier scene's black overhead plane and blown-out opening may be different failures.** The large clean-edged black polygon could be a present roof/ceiling mesh with an incorrect normal, material or unlit underside; it could also be a missing/incorrect render pass. The white exterior opening could mean the expected wall/window geometry is absent, or simply an exposure/tonemap/fog failure. Compare color and depth captures before calling either a culling or lighting bug.
- **The strong blur and red/cyan edge fringes point toward post-processing as a separate suspect.** They may be intended depth-of-field/chromatic-aberration effects exaggerated by incorrect inputs, or a post-process shader/format/composition problem. If the scene target is sharp and correctly exposed but the presented image is not, trace the final passes; do not fold that symptom into the indirect-lighting hypothesis.
- **Actor and counter textures now appearing do not rule out texture-streaming errors.** Earlier captures and the log show texture updates during play, but the cashier screenshot alone cannot distinguish valid low-resolution mips from stale or incorrectly selected views.
- **Performance is not just GPU shader time.** The samples nearest the screenshot have widely varying draws and GPU time. Preparation/copy-back and scene workload are material; a lighting change may improve the image while making frame time worse.

### Should ray tracing be enabled?

**Do not enable the dynamic BVH builder in the current run.** The current branch's `VICTORY_BVH_BUILDER=1` switch applies to dynamic acceleration-structure construction, not to the static-world shadow tracing feature the game uses for its shipped structures. Builder stages run all-or-none because partial writes can corrupt title memory. The source keeps the builder off unless every created builder pipeline is ready; a previous partial-builder run crashed the title. Preserve the cashier progress and do not restart this session just to set that variable.

**A static-world shadow-tracing experiment is a reasonable next visual test, on a separate branch/build.** PR #156 is still an open candidate in the public fork. Its author reports that, with the builder off, static-world ray-traced shadows appeared on an RTX 3090; four of five shadow-ray programs translate on wave32, while the adaptive trace and irradiance-cache shading remain gaps. The same report explicitly says turning the dynamic builder on raised a roughly 0.3-second frame to about 4 seconds and produced a washed-out white scene. That supports testing static shadow traces without the builder, but it does not promise bounced light or fix the cashier post-process/geometry issues on this RTX 5080. Check the current PR head for its follow-up lane-uniform correctness fix before using it. [RT PR #156](https://github.com/fxceless/victory-fork/pull/156) · [Fork RT design notes](../fork-ray-tracing.md)
